STEP 1 — UIDAI PUBLIC CERTIFICATE

Load UIDAI's public certificate once. Download it from uidai.gov.in (Aadhaar Secure QR / offline eKYC documentation) and select the .cer or .pem file here. It stays in this browser.

STEP 2 — READ THE QR

Scan the Secure QR on the Aadhaar card with your camera, or upload a photo of it. The QR is dense, so a sharp, close, well-lit image works best.
