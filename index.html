STEP 1 — SIGNING CERTIFICATE none loaded

Load UIDAI's public certificate (.cer / .pem) from uidai.gov.in. For a demonstration without a real card, load the built-in synthetic test certificate instead — it is not UIDAI's key and is clearly labelled as such.

STEP 2 — READ THE QR

Scan the Secure QR on the physical Aadhaar card, upload a sharp photo of it, or upload the e-Aadhaar PDF. The QR is very dense — fill the frame, avoid glare, hold steady. Photocopies and photos of screens usually fail to decode.
