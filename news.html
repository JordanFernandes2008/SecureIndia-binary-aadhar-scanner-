        How to read a result

        Corroborated
        an official source or several established outlets report it independently.
        Reported
        several outlets carry it, but none we treat as established.
        Disputed
        some coverage says it is false or misleading.
        False
        a fact-checker, official source or established outlet has called it fake.
        Unverified
        too little evidence either way. Don't share it yet.

        The verdict summarises the evidence; it is not a final ruling. How often something is forwarded proves nothing, and one official denial outweighs a thousand shares. Always open the sources.
